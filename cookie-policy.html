COOKIE OVERVIEW

What Are Cookies and Why Do We Use Them?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember useful information, improve functionality, enhance security, analyze performance, and personalize certain aspects of your browsing experience.

🍪 DESIGNED TO IMPROVE YOUR EXPERIENCE

Designed to Improve Your Experience

Cookies generally do not identify users personally on their own, but they help websites remember preferences, maintain secure sessions, optimize performance, and understand how visitors interact with the website to make continuous improvements.
